# Redaction checklist

Before publishing, confirm none of these are in the repo:

- customer emails, first or last names, order IDs, shipping addresses
- Klaviyo, Meta, PostHog, Shopify or Apps Script keys and tokens
- supplier names, unit costs, margins
- raw CSV exports (ignored by .gitignore)
- Klaviyo list, segment and campaign IDs (kept out of the README on purpose)
